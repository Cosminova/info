Privacy

Privacy policy

Cosminova collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting, and the app makes no network requests of its own. The rest of this page is that same sentence with the specifics behind it, because a policy that only says "we respect your privacy" is not checkable.

What the app collects

Nothing. No personal information, no usage statistics, no device identifiers, no advertising identifiers, no location. There is no sign-in, so there is no name or email address to collect in the first place.

Cosminova contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code of any kind. Nothing about what you look at, search for or travel to leaves your device, because there is nothing in the app that sends it anywhere.

What is stored on your own device

Your settings — camera mode, which panels are open, label density, whether the music is on, and the bookmarks you save. These are written to your device's own storage inside the app's sandbox container so the app opens the way you left it. They never leave the machine, and deleting the app deletes them.

Network access

The app does not connect to any server operated by us, because there isn't one. Every catalogue, texture and elevation model is inside the application bundle, which is why it works with the network off entirely.

macOS: The Mac version does hold the standard outgoing-network entitlement. That is a technical requirement of the system web view the renderer runs inside, which will not start without it; it is not used to send anything.

iOS: The iOS version includes the necessary network permissions, which is a standard requirement for apps on iOS. These are not used to send any personal data anywhere.

If you follow a link out of the app — to a catalogue or a data source — that link opens in your own browser, and from there your browser's terms apply rather than ours.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, and the Windows installer is served from GitHub Releases. GitHub records ordinary web-server information when a page or file is requested, such as your IP address and browser, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not add any tracking of our own: there are no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts on this site.

Buying and downloading from the App Store

Purchases and downloads are handled entirely by Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. Apple gives developers aggregated, anonymised reports — how many downloads happened in a country, for instance — and nothing in them identifies an individual. We receive no personal data about you from Apple, and we cannot tell who has installed the app.

This applies to both the macOS App Store and the iOS App Store.

Children

Cosminova is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, of any age. There is no chat, no user-generated content and no advertising in it.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, deletion and portability apply to data that has been collected. Since none has been, there is nothing held about you to request, correct, export or delete. If you would like that confirmed in writing for your own records, ask and it will be confirmed.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects anything, this page will say what and why before that version ships, and the date below will change. It will not be quietly amended.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in the app, can be raised at github.com/Cosminova/info/issues. See the support page for help with the app itself.
