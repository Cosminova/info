Gliese test-world ground materials use Grass004 and Ground054 from ambientCG
by Lennart Demes, licensed CC0 1.0 Universal.

Sources:
https://ambientcg.com/a/Grass004
https://ambientcg.com/a/Ground054
https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/

Source archives: Grass004_2K-JPG.zip and Ground054_2K-JPG.zip.
Albedo maps are converted to WebP at quality 94. biome-normal.webp losslessly
packs the OpenGL normal maps: RG = Grass004 XY; BA = Ground054 XY.
These assets are artistic materials, not imagery of Gliese 12 b.
